PP-OCRv5 mobile text recognition models

The models in bin/_internal/ppocr_addon/models/ (rec.onnx and dict.txt for
each script) are PaddlePaddle's PP-OCRv5 mobile recognition models:

  Copyright (c) PaddlePaddle Authors
  Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (LICENSE in this folder)
  https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR

converted from Paddle's format to ONNX by RapidAI's RapidOCR project
(Apache-2.0), as published on ModelScope, RapidAI/RapidOCR, tag v3.10.0:

  https://www.modelscope.cn/models/RapidAI/RapidOCR
  onnx/PP-OCRv5/rec/<script>_PP-OCRv5_rec_mobile.onnx
  paddle/PP-OCRv5/rec/<script>_PP-OCRv5_rec_mobile/<dictionary>.txt

The files are unchanged; tools/fetch_models.py in the add-on's repository
pins each one by sha256.
